Western & Gongfu Preparation

To appreciate the full depth of high mountain Ceylon tea, use soft mineral water heated to the exact target temperature. Never allow boiling water to scorch delicate tips.

Step 1: Warm Teaware

Rinse your porcelain or glass teapot with hot water to maintain brewing temperature stability.

Step 2: Gentle Infusion

Pour fresh heated water over the leaves in a circular motion. Cover and steep without stirring.

Step 3: Decant Fully

Pour out every drop to prevent over-extraction. High quality orthodox leaves can be re-steeped.
