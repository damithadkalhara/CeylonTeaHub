Why True Ceylon Tea is the World's Finest Liquor

1

Hand-Plucked Two Leaves & A Bud

Unlike mechanized harvesting that cuts thick stems and woody stalks, our estate pickers delicately harvest only the tender shoot containing the highest density of flavonoids and L-theanine.

2

Gentle Mountain Sunlight Withering

Freshly picked leaves rest in ambient mountain air withering troughs for 14-18 hours, naturally reducing moisture and developing delicate floral and fruity enzyme precursors.

3

Precision Orthodox Rolling & Fermentation

Traditional brass roller tables twist the leaf without pulverizing it, releasing natural juices for controlled oxidation monitored by generational tea masters.
