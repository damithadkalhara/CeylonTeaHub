The Genesis

James Taylor & The Loolecondera Miracle

In 1867, a young Scotsman named James Taylor planted 19 acres of tea seedlings on the Loolecondera Estate in Kandy. When the devastating coffee rust fungus wiped out Ceylon's coffee plantations, Taylor demonstrated that Sri Lanka's high misty ridges and rich soils were uniquely suited for cultivating superior *Camellia sinensis*.

Taylor engineered the very first orthodox withering and rolling techniques on the verandah of his estate bungalow. By 1893 at the Chicago World's Fair, Ceylon Tea was celebrated internationally for its luminous copper liqueur and unmatched briskness.

Today, **Ceylon Tea Hub** honors this unbroken lineage by partnering directly with historic single estates, ensuring that every batch bypasses commodity middlemen and reaches global connoisseurs at peak botanical freshness.

Uncompromised Craft

The 5 Steps of Orthodox Tea Making

While mass-market tea uses aggressive CTC machines that crush leaf into dust, pure Ceylon tea uses the delicate 5-step orthodox methodology.

1

Delicate Hand Plucking

Only the tender terminal bud and two juvenile leaves are hand-picked at dawn to preserve essential catechins.

2

Ambient Air Withering

Leaves rest on wooden troughs for 14-18 hours, losing 50% moisture and activating aromatic enzymes.

3

Orthodox Table Rolling

Brass rollers gently twist the whole leaf without chopping, releasing natural cell saps for oxidation.

4

Master Fermentation

Monitored closely on cooling tiles. The leaf turns from vibrant green to rich coppery red under exact humidity.

5

Firing & Optical Sorting

Hot air firing arrests oxidation, locking in flavor. Whole leaves are meticulously graded by size and tip density.

The Official Lion of Ceylon

Owned and regulated by the Sri Lanka Tea Board, the Lion Logo is the world's most rigorous geographical indication seal.

1. 100% Pure Ceylon

Must contain solely tea grown and harvested in Sri Lanka. Blending with cheaper imported teas is strictly prohibited.

2. Packed at Source

Must be packaged in Sri Lanka into final consumer tins or pouches to prevent degradation during ocean transit.

3. Chemical & Ozone Friendly

Ceylon Tea was the first agricultural product globally certified Ozone Friendly (Montreal Protocol compliant).

4. Ethical Living Wages

Guarantees fair compensation, free healthcare, maternity leave, and child education for estate worker communities.
